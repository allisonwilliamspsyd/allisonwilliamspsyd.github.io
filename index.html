Selected work

01 / Integrated care

Turning a care model into everyday practice

Bringing primary and behavioral healthcare together requires changes in clinical practice, staffing, reporting, and governance. I led multidisciplinary implementation at B & D Integrated Health Services, building on the 2016 primary-care launch.

The organization achieved NCQA CCBHC accreditation in January 2026 and HRSA Health Center Look-Alike designation effective April 1, 2026.

02 / Patient flow

Making the next step in care clear

During my TelePsych Supports leadership work with WakeMed, I helped standardize behavioral-health disposition categories and reassessment processes, and partnered with the Epic team on patient tracking.

These changes contributed to broader strategies associated with a reduction in boarded/admitted behavioral-health patients from approximately 80–100 to 20–40 across campuses, sustained over several months.

03 / Practical AI

Giving time back to the team

A weekly financial report required six staff-hours to prepare. I built AI-assisted Excel VBA automation across two EHR reporting streams, standardized payer and service categories, and corrected reporting distortions.

Preparation fell to approximately 30 minutes. I also developed and piloted AI tools supporting assessment, care-plan, and progress-note review, using clinician feedback and repeat testing to refine them.

04 / Workforce planning

Connecting demand with clinical capacity

I built workforce models and Monday.com workflows connecting member assignments, acuity-based outreach, quality tasks, productivity, and capacity. Directors and supervisors used these measures to support daily oversight and hiring decisions.
